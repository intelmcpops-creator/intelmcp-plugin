About this panel

This page calls IntelMCP through your own IntelMCP connection, so it shows your organization's alerts, rules and settings, and the collection your plan includes. The first time, Claude asks whether the page may use IntelMCP. It changes or deletes something only when you press a button or key; deleting and rotating a secret ask you to confirm. Triage changes wait under Changes until you press Apply, which sends them in as few calls as possible (at most two for a page of alerts, plus one per kind of exclusion). Webhook secrets are shown here once: copy them before you leave the page. Times are in UTC.
