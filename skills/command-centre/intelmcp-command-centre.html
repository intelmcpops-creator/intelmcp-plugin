About this panel

This page calls IntelMCP through your own IntelMCP connection, so it shows your alerts, rules and settings, and the collection your plan includes. The first time, Claude asks whether the page may use IntelMCP. It changes or deletes something only when you press a button or key; deleting and rotating a secret ask you to confirm. A verdict, an acknowledgement or a hidden channel, country, topic or phrase is saved when you press it, in one call; Undo (or the z key) puts back the last verdict or acknowledgement. Hiding alerts asks you once more first. Webhook secrets are shown here once: copy them before you leave the page. Times are in UTC.
